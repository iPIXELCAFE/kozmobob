· · · FREQUENTLY ASKED · · ·
CAN I SEE MY AD BEFORE IT GOES LIVE?
Yes. Send your creative before payment and we'll mock it up in the actual billboard position so you can approve it first.
WHAT IF FEWER THAN 5 BRANDS SHARE MY COMMUNITY SLOT?
You get proportionally more screen time — no price change. If you're the only brand in that hour you get 100% of that slot for $25. We don't top up to 5 artificially.
DO YOU OFFER MONTHLY PACKAGES?
Yes, for premium placements we negotiate monthly rates with a discount. Email us with your intended volume and we'll put together a package.
IS THERE A CONTRACT?
Yes. Every booking includes a simple one-page advertising agreement covering placement, duration, pricing, content rules, and our reporting commitment. Sent by email before payment.
