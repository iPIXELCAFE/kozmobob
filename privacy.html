1. Who We Are

KozmoBob ("we", "us") operates the website kozmobob.com — a free entertainment platform offering horoscopes, tarot readings, and cosmic messages. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our site.

2. What We Collect

We collect as little as possible. Specifically:

  • Device ID — a random anonymous identifier stored in your browser to remember your daily free reading limits. This never leaves your device except to our reading history storage.
  • Reading history — if you tap "My Readings", your readings are saved anonymously by device ID using Upstash (our data provider). No name, email, or account required.
  • Cookies — we and our advertising partner (Google) use cookies. See section 4.
  • Usage data — standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected automatically by our hosting provider (Vercel).

We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information. No sign-up is required to use KozmoBob.

3. How We Use Data

  • To enforce daily free reading limits per device
  • To display your saved readings when you request them
  • To serve relevant advertisements (via Google AdSense)
  • To monitor site performance and fix errors

We do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Cookies & Advertising

KozmoBob uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to show ads based on your browsing history across the web. This is standard practice on virtually all free websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to KozmoBob and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data, visit: google.com/policies.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Vercel — website hosting (privacy policy)
  • Upstash — reading history storage (privacy policy)
  • Groq — AI oracle responses (privacy policy)
  • Google AdSense — advertising (privacy policy)

6. Data Retention

Anonymous reading history is stored for up to 90 days. Server logs are retained by Vercel per their standard policy. Cookie data is managed by your browser and Google per their policies.

7. Your Rights

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's cookies and local storage. Since we don't collect names or emails, there is no personal account to delete.

EU/EEA users have rights under GDPR including access, rectification, and erasure of personal data. Since we store only anonymous device IDs, there is effectively no personal data linked to you.

8. Children

KozmoBob is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, you can reach us at: privacy@kozmobob.com
